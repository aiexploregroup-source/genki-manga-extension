げんき GENKI MANGA

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Genki Manga — Japanese Study New Tab is a local study tool. We respect your privacy and collect nothing.

1. Data we collect

None. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information, browsing history, search queries, or study data. There are no analytics, trackers, or remote servers operated by this extension.

2. Data stored on your device

The extension saves only your study progress and preferences in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local): XP, streak, completed days, task checkboxes, reminder time, chosen background, mascot, and your optional display name. You can erase all of it at any time with ⚙ Settings → Reset all progress, or by removing the extension.

3. Permissions we use

  • storage — to remember your progress and settings locally.
  • alarms — to fire your chosen daily study reminder.
  • notifications — to show that reminder. Clicking it opens your study tab.
  • New tab override — to display the manga study dashboard instead of the default new tab page.

4. Internet access

The extension itself works offline. When you are online, Google Fonts may load for nicer typography, and actions you initiate — typing in the search box, or clicking "Genki Source" / "Open full page" — open Google, Jisho, or the external Genki I study site in a new tab. Those sites have their own privacy policies. Japanese pronunciation uses your device's built-in text-to-speech voices; no audio is uploaded.

5. Children

This extension is safe for learners of all ages and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here and in the extension repository.

7. Contact

Questions or feedback: open an issue at github.com/aiexploregroup-source/genki-manga-extension/issues.
