## Full description (paste into the store)

Turn every new tab into a colorful manga study session for Japanese!

GENKI MANGA follows the げんき Genki I — 30-Day Manga Study Challenge: a 30-stage quest from hiragana and greetings through Lessons 1-12 to the Final Boss review. It is colorful, fast, and works completely offline.

★ MANGA / ANIME DESIGN
- Halftone dots, speed lines, speech bubbles, bold outlines and sticker-style buttons
- Four hand-drawn anime scenes: Sakura Street, Sunset City, Torii Shrine and Neon Tokyo, with animated petals and sparkles
- Three adorable chibi study buddies (Hana, Ken and Mochi) who give you Japanese pep talks with text-to-speech
- Live clock, Japanese greeting by time of day, and Google search (type "j: word" to search Jisho)

★ LEARN JAPANESE
- Exact 30-day Genki I map: Intro, Lessons 1-12, review checkpoints and finale
- 210 vocabulary items with romaji and example sentences, 76 grammar points and 90 quiz questions
- Full hiragana and katakana charts - click any kana to hear its pronunciation
- Flip flashcards by day, or use the mega deck of all 30 days
- 10-question Boss Quiz mixing lesson questions with generated vocab questions; score 6+ to clear your mission
- Each day opens a lesson panel with vocabulary, grammar and a link to the full Genki source book

★ MOTIVATION THAT STICKS
- Four-part daily mission: look, say, write, quiz - then stamp the stage for +50 XP
- XP, date-aware streak, six ranks from Rookie Gakusei to Genki Legend, and a 30-day progress map
- Daily reminder you control: set the time, toggle it on/off, or send a test notification. Clicking the reminder opens your study tab
- Toolbar popup with today's mission, progress, reminder settings and a mini quiz

★ PRIVACY
No accounts, no analytics, no data collection. Progress stays in your browser's local storage. See the privacy policy link below.

Install: open a new tab and start Day 1. がんばって！
